# The economic burden of chronic migraine in Freedonia, 2023: a prevalence-based cost-of-illness study

L. Fabricated and M. Hypothetical — Freedonian Institute for Health Economics (fictional)

## Abstract

**Objective.** To estimate the annual economic burden of chronic migraine among Freedonian adults in 2023 from a societal perspective.
**Methods.** Prevalence-based cost-of-illness study combining national survey data on prevalence and resource use with unit costs; productivity losses valued with the human-capital method; health losses expressed as disability-adjusted life years (DALYs).
**Results.** Chronic migraine cost F$ 2.31 billion in 2023 (F$ 6,940 per person with the condition); 64 % of the total was productivity loss.
**Conclusion.** Chronic migraine imposes a large economic burden on Freedonian society.

## 1. Introduction

Chronic migraine (15 or more headache days per month) affects an estimated 2.1 % of Freedonian adults (fictional). No national estimate of its cost exists. This study quantifies the burden to inform priority setting in the national neurology strategy.

## 2. Methods

### 2.1 Population and data

Prevalence by age and sex came from the 2023 Freedonian Health Interview Survey (fictional; n = 18,400 adults). Resource use (general practitioner and neurologist visits, emergency visits, admissions, acute and preventive medication) and days of work lost were taken from the survey's migraine module.

### 2.2 Perspective and costs

A societal perspective was adopted to capture both health-care costs and productivity losses. Health-care resources were valued with the 2023 national tariff (Freedonian dollars, F$, 2023 prices). Productivity losses were valued with the human-capital method at the 2023 average gross wage. Costs of future lost productivity due to early retirement were discounted at 3 % per year.

### 2.3 Health losses

Disability-adjusted life years were calculated with the disability weight for chronic migraine from the Global Burden of Disease study.

### 2.4 Sensitivity analysis

One-way sensitivity analyses varied prevalence (±25 %), unit costs (±20 %) and the productivity valuation method (friction-cost method instead of human capital).

## 3. Results

| Cost category | F$ million | % |
|---|---|---|
| Primary care visits | 141 | 6 |
| Neurology visits | 96 | 4 |
| Emergency visits and admissions | 205 | 9 |
| Medication | 390 | 17 |
| Productivity losses | 1,478 | 64 |
| **Total** | **2,310** | 100 |

Chronic migraine caused 41,200 DALYs in 2023. With the friction-cost method the total fell to F$ 1.12 billion; with prevalence ±25 % it ranged from F$ 1.73 to 2.89 billion.

## 4. Discussion

Chronic migraine is a costly condition in Freedonia, mostly through lost productivity. The estimates depend strongly on the productivity valuation method. Limitations include self-reported resource use and the absence of data on informal care. Future work should evaluate interventions that could reduce this burden.
