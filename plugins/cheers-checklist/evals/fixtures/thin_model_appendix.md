## A1. Currency, price date and conversion

All costs are expressed in Northshire dollars (N$, fictional) at 2023 prices. Unit costs are taken from the 2023 regional unit cost tariff; resource quantities (number of calls, home visits, clinic visits, readmission days and residential-care weeks) come from the 2021–2022 orthopaedic follow-up audit. No currency conversion was required.
